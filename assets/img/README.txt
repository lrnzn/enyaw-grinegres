Put the school logo here as logo.png (or logo.jpg / logo.webp / logo.svg). It appears on the sidebar, the sign-in page and printed sheets.

Login page photo: save a photo of the school as login-bg.jpg (or .png / .webp) in this folder. It appears on the left side of the login page, slightly blurred and darkened. A wide photo, at least 1600 pixels across, works best. Without it the page uses the green background.
